Panchang – Privacy Policy

Effective date: 23 November 2026
App: Panchang (Vedic Panchang & Rashifal), package com.jyotish.panchang_app

Panchang is a daily Vedic calendar app for India. This policy explains what the app does and does not do with your information.

Information stored only on your device

The city, language, Moon sign (rashi), optional birth date and birth time, reminder times, theme and similar choices you make in the app are stored only on your phone. Birth details are used on the device to work out your Moon sign and are never uploaded to us. We do not ask you to create an account.

Location

The app does not use GPS or read your device location. You pick a city from a built-in list of Indian cities.

Information the app fetches

To show the day's Rashifal text, the app reads public content from Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore). This request is a normal internet request, so Google receives technical data such as your IP address. We do not send your city, Moon sign or birth details with it. If you are offline, the app shows built-in text instead.

Advertising

The free version shows banner ads served by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect and use your device's advertising ID, IP address and general device information to show ads and prevent fraud. Ad content is limited to a general-audience rating. See Google's advertising policy. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in Android Settings > Privacy > Ads. Buying the one-time Remove ads upgrade turns ads off in the app.

Purchases

The Remove ads upgrade is processed by Google Play. We receive only confirmation that the purchase was made. We never see or store your payment details.

Notifications

Daily reminders are scheduled on your device using local alarms. You can switch them off in the app or in Android settings. The app asks for permission to post notifications, and for a boot-completed signal so that reminders keep working after you restart your phone.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell your personal information.
  • We do not collect your name, phone number, email address, contacts, photos or files.
  • We do not use your information to build a profile of you.

Children

The app is for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Entertainment disclaimer

Panchang timings are calculated with standard astronomical algorithms and may differ by a few minutes from printed almanacs, and festival days can differ by tradition. Rashifal guidance is for information and entertainment only and is not medical, legal, financial or professional advice.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version is always on this page.
